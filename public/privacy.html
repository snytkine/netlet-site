Privacy Policy

NetLet chat · Effective November 8, 2026

In short: NetLet does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your messages travel only between computers on your own local network, and everything the app keeps is stored on your own computer.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to the NetLet chat desktop app for macOS and Windows, and to this website (netlet.app). NetLet is a chat app for people on the same local network (for example the same Wi-Fi or office network). One person hosts, and the others join by entering the host's address.

Data we collect

None. We do not run any server that receives data from the app. We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. We do not use analytics, telemetry, remote logging, advertising or third-party tracking tools in the app.

How your chat works

  • Chat messages are sent directly from your computer to the other computers in the chat. They are never sent to a server on the Internet.
  • The computer that hosts the chat helps the others find each other. It does not relay or store your messages.
  • NetLet only connects to addresses on your private local network. It ignores public Internet addresses.
  • The people you chat with receive your messages and the display name you chose, and their app keeps a copy on their own computer. Only chat with people you are happy to share your messages with.

What stays on your computer

The app stores the following in a database on your own computer. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

  • Your message history (messages you sent and received).
  • Your settings and the display name you chose.
  • A random identifier that the app creates for your installation so that other chat members can recognise it.
  • Networks you used before and the addresses of the computers you connected to, so that NetLet can offer to reconnect. This can include details that help recognise a network, such as its address range and Wi-Fi name. These details are used only on your computer and are never sent to other people or to us.
  • An unsent message draft, so you do not lose it if you leave the chat screen.

You can delete your message history at any time with Clear message history in the app's Settings. Uninstalling the app and removing its data folder deletes everything it stored.

Permissions

To work, NetLet needs to make and accept connections on your local network. On macOS the system asks you to allow Local Network access, and when you host, the system may ask whether to allow incoming connections. NetLet does not ask for access to your files, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Internet connections

  • Mac App Store edition: makes no Internet connections at all. Apple updates it through the App Store.
  • Downloadable edition (Windows and the direct macOS download): can check whether a newer version exists. It makes one request to the public GitHub Releases page of NetLet when the app starts and every few hours after that. The request contains no chat content, no names and no identifiers, and no version number or platform. As with any web request, GitHub can see your IP address. You can turn this check off in Settings, under Options. The app does not download or install anything by itself.
  • This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics on it. GitHub, as the host, may process standard web request data under its own privacy statement.

Children

NetLet does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts and no way to contact strangers: you can only chat with people who are on your local network and who know the host's address.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/snytkine/netlet-site/issues. Please do not post private information there.
